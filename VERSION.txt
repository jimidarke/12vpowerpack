## 0.3.1
- Surge clamp decided: SMCJ18CA; a jump start or load dump blows the input fuse
- Parts chosen and checked against datasheets: FETs, Zeners, diodes, charge resistor and fuse, fuse holders, inductor, DS18B20, SK6812
- Added: two INA226 current monitors (LCSC), single SK6812 status LED, hold-up timer semantics (0 = no timeout, minimum 1 min)
- Barrel jack PJ-063AH accepted at its −25 °C rating
- `netlist.md`: pin-by-pin net table for hand capture in Flux; 15 new datasheet extracts

## 0.3.0
- Requirements and architecture v3: vehicle-only 12 V AGM unit, battery floated on the car feed through a charge resistor
- Dropped: lithium build, charger IC, USB-C PD input, 12 V converter, display
- 12 V output unregulated (3 A continuous, 5 A peak); hardware low-voltage cutoff from the 5 V buck's enable and power-good pins
- XIAO ESP32-C6 kept for the adjustable ignition-off timer, voltage and battery temperature reporting
- Two INA226 current monitors: power in from the car feed and power out to the loads
- v2 block briefs moved to `blocks/retired/`; Flux log: second block 1 run and the scope change recorded
